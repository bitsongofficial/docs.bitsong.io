# How to Add $BTSG Liquidity to Osmosis Pools

**1.** Go to [**https://osmosis.zone**](https://osmosis.zone)

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**2.** Click on "_Connect Wallet_" to connect your Keplr wallet to Osmosis.

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**3.** Enter your Keplr password and click to "_Unlock_"

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**4.** Click on "_Assets_" in the left navigation menu and find $BTSG on the list.  Now click on "_Deposit_" to transfer your $BTSG from your BitSong Wallet to **Osmosis**.

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**5.** Select the amount of $BTSG you want to deposit on **Osmosis** and click to "_Approve_" the transaction from **Keplr**. Now you can see your $BTSG balance on the "_Assets_" page.

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**6.** Now it's time to select the pool to which you want to add liquidity. You can find **three** different BTSG pools: Pool #**574** $ATOM/$BTSG - Pool #**573** $BTSG/$OSMO and Pool #**592** $UST/$BTSG.

**In this guide, we will add liquidity to Pool #573 $BTSG/$OSMO but the principles are the same for all Osmosis BTSG pools.**

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**7.**  Click on "_Add / Remove Liquidity"_

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**8.** Now you have to pair the amount of tokens you want to add. In this case, we have chosen to add $OSMO and $BTSG. Choose the amount and "_Approve_" from Keplr.

Now you have added liquidity to the pool!

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**9.**  If you want to bond your Liquidity Provider tokens (LP tokens) to earn rewards, click the _"Start Earning"_ button_._

<!-- TODO: add image -->

**10.** Choose the Unbonding period, click on "_Max"_ to bond all of your LP tokens, and then click _"Bond."_ You'll be prompted to confirm the transaction on Keplr.

<!-- TODO: add image -->

That's it! You're now earning rewards for bonding your LP tokens.
