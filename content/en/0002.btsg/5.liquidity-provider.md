## Becoming a BTSG Liquidity Provider

### Why become a BTSG Liquidity Provider?

::tip
BTSG liquidity providers play a crucial role in ensuring a continuous supply of liquidity for BTSG on decentralized exchanges. In return for depositing into BTSG pools, liquidity providers earn a share of :icon{name="i-lucide-percent"} **transaction fees** paid by traders who use the pool to swap BTSG tokens.
::

### Provide liquidity to BTSG Pools on Osmosis

These instructions are for mainnet-BTSG tokens.

::note
To add liquidity to BTSG pools on Osmosis you'll need to have installed the :icon{name="i-lucide-shield-check"} **Keplr wallet**. You'll also need to have a balance of the equivalent pairs for which you want to add liquidity, e.g. BTSG and ATOM.
::

::steps{level="4"}

#### Navigate to the Pools section

Go to the ["Pools" section on the Osmosis app](https://app.osmosis.zone/pools){target="_blank"}. Scroll down until you find the BTSG pools.

#### Add liquidity

Select **"Add/Remove Liquidity"**. Input the BTSG you want to add, and your other token balance will be calculated automatically.

#### Approve the transaction

Click the **"Add Liquidity"** button and when prompted, approve the transaction in your Keplr wallet. You will receive :icon{name="i-lucide-ticket"} **LP tokens** to represent your staked liquidity.

#### Withdraw your tokens

When you wish to withdraw your tokens, navigate back to the Pools section on Osmosis with your Keplr wallet connected, and you can use the **"Add/Remove Liquidity"** option to redeem LP tokens.

::

::callout{icon="i-lucide-book-open" color="neutral" to="https://docs.bitsong.io/useful-guides/how-to-add-usdbtsg-liquidity-in-osmosis-pools" target="_blank"}
A short guide on how to add BTSG liquidity in Osmosis pools can also be found **here**.
::

### Risks of liquidity pools

::caution
Becoming a liquidity provider comes with a certain amount of risk. Please ensure you research and understand these risks before depositing your funds into a liquidity pool.
::

::card-group

::card
---
title: Impermanent Loss
icon: i-lucide-trending-down
---
Cryptocurrencies are volatile assets and when prices for tokens in a pool diverge, the liquidity provider may incur impermanent loss.
::

::card
---
title: Smart Contract Risk
icon: i-lucide-file-code
---
Decentralized finance protocols can also be subject to other risks, such as smart contract risk.
::
