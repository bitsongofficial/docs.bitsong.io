# Sinfonia

[**Sinfonia**](https://app.sinfonia.zone/) is a cutting-edge dApp (Decentralised Application) that enables users to create and manage music FanTokens and NFTs on the [**BitSong**](https://bitsong.io/) and Osmosis Blockchains. With its user-friendly platform, Sinfonia is revolutionizing the music industry by bridging the gap between **Web2** and **Web3** technologies, empowering artists and music industry players with a range of innovative tools to enhance their projects.
